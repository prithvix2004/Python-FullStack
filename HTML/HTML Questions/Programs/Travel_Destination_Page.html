Travel Destinations

Seven Places to visit and explore..


1. Lakshadweep

Lakshadweep is home to serene beaches. You can expect summer heat and the temperature rising upto 32 degree Celsius. Lakshadweep is also very popular among relaxation - seekers.


2. Bali

If you are a foodie, head straight to Bali. You will love sightseeing and water sports. You can also explore the beautiful beaches of Bali.


3. Istanbul

Istanbul is a destination in heritage. You will love exploring the sites and shopping from the colourful markets. You can also cuisines at Istanbul.


4. Mykonos

An intrinsic part of Cyclades Islands in the Aegean Sea, the Greek Island of Mykonos is all about age - old churches.


5. Goa

Rev up your spirits with the stunning adventures of Goa. You can expect heavy rainfall throughout the season, pleasant sea breeze. Humidity tends to be high. You can also sample delicious cuisines at Goa.


6. Alleppey

Take your beach love to Alleppey. You can expect heavy rainfalls, along with humid weather. You can also unwind amidst the tranquil surroundings of Alleppey.


7. Varkala

Come and fall in love with the beach vibe of Varkala. You can expect heavy to moderate rainfalls. Varkala is also very popular among relaxation - seekers.
